Who We Are

7 Points Properties & Development Limited

is registered as a Limited Liability Company under the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) with RC: 1827181. We are a premier real estate development company that brings an advanced and innovative approach to real estate development, combining technical expertise with a deep understanding of market dynamics.

We provide solutions to real estate and development challenges through an innovation approach that is quality-driven and client-centric. Our company is built on a partner-relationship approach, ensuring that every client receives personalized attention and tailored solutions.

We are distinctive for our ability to deliver superior value to our customers, investors, and employees. Our commitment to excellence extends across all aspects of our business, from design and construction to after-sales service and customer support.

Our Core Values

We are guided by five core pillars that drive everything we do

Socio-Cultural Value

We create developments that respect and enhance the cultural fabric of communities. Our projects are designed with consideration for local traditions and social dynamics.

Experimental Value

We embrace innovation and new approaches to real estate development. We are not afraid to experiment with new designs, materials, and construction techniques.

Building-Technical Value

We prioritize structural integrity, sound construction practices, and technical excellence. Our buildings are engineered to last with attention to every structural detail.

Economic Value

We deliver projects that offer excellent value for money. Smart planning, efficient use of resources, and competitive pricing ensure your investment grows.

Sales & After-Sales Service

Our commitment doesn't end at project completion. We provide comprehensive after-sales support, maintenance guidance, and ongoing customer service.

Quality & Excellence

Quality is non-negotiable. We maintain rigorous quality control standards at every stage of development, from planning through delivery and beyond.

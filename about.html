Who we are

A real estate solutions provider.

7 Points Properties & Development Limited is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission under RC: 1827181 and was established in 2021. We provide real estate solutions through an advanced and innovative approach to development.

Our partner-relationship approach is shaped by socio-cultural, experimental, building-technical, and economic values. We support our customers from sales through after-sales service, staying close to the details that make every project work.

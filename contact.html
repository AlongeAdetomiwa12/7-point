Frequently Asked Questions

What is your project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. We provide detailed schedules during the consultation phase. Most projects are completed within 12-24 months.

Do you offer consultation services?

Yes! We provide free initial consultations to discuss your project needs, budget, and timeline. Contact us to schedule a meeting.

What payment options do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, installment plans, and flexible payment schemes. We'll discuss the best option for your situation during consultation.

Do you handle renovation projects?

Absolutely! We specialize in both new construction and renovation/restoration projects. Our team has extensive experience with building rehabilitation.

Can you work within my budget?

Yes. We work with projects of all scales and budgets. Our value engineering approach helps maximize your investment while maintaining quality standards.

What areas do you service?

We primarily operate in Lagos and Ogun State, but we can discuss projects in other regions. Contact us to explore possibilities.
